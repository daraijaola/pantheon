// How it works \\

Buys build the basket.
Sells burn the supply.

Every PNTH trade pays a small fee. Here is where it goes.

Every trade pays 1%. Half goes to Ascend, half to PANTHEON.

Buys fill the basket. Our half buys Ascend's 10 best coins, locked for stakers.

Sells burn PNTH. Our half of every sell is destroyed. Fewer PNTH, forever.

No one can change these rules. Not even us.
